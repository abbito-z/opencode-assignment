Origami for Beginners

Fun paper folding you can try today!

What is Origami?

Origami is the Japanese art of paper folding. Starting with a single sheet of square paper, you can create animals, flowers, and geometric shapes without using glue or scissors. It's a wonderful activity for developing patience, spatial reasoning, and creativity. All ages can enjoy the art!

Basic Folds

Origami has 2 basic folds that are the building blocks for most models.

  • Valley Fold: With the paper on a flat surface, bring the bottom edge to the top. The fold should create a V shape. It looks like shape of a valley.
  • Mountain Fold: With the paper on a flat surface, bring the top edge to the bottom. The fold should create and Λ shape. It looks like the peak of a mountain.

Origami Bases

Many models start with a base. Once you fold the base, you make the specific folds for your model. All of these are created by simple mountain and valley folds.

  • Square Base: The foundation for many traditional models.
  • Bird Base: This builds on the square base and is used for bird shaped origami models.
  • Ballon Base: This base is perfect for inflatable models like the waterbomb and some flowers

Getting Started

Here are a few tips for your origami journey:

  • Use square paper — origami paper is ideal, but you can trim any sheet.
  • Work on a flat, clean surface.
  • Make crisp folds by running your fingernail. Crisp folds help the model stay structured
  • Be patient, each fold builds toward the final model. Folding origami is the art of patience and trusting the process!
