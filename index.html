What is Origami?

Origami is the Japanese art of paper folding. Starting with a single sheet of square paper, you can create animals, flowers, and geometric shapes without using glue or scissors. It's a wonderful activity for developing patience, spatial reasoning, and creativity — and it's fun for all ages!

Basic Folds

Start with these fundamental folds that form the building blocks of most origami models.

  • Valley Fold: Fold the paper towards you, creating a valley.
  • Mountain Fold: Fold the paper away from you, creating a peak.
  • Square Base: The foundation for many traditional models.
  • Bird Base: A classic fold used in many bird-shaped designs.

Getting Started

Here are a few tips for your origami journey:

  • Use square paper — origami paper is ideal, but you can trim any sheet.
  • Work on a flat, clean surface.
  • Make crisp folds by running your fingernail or a bone folder along the crease.
  • Be patient — each fold builds toward the final model!
